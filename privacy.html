Privacy Policy — Tailor: ATS Resume Builder

Effective date: October 22, 2026
Contact: xuanhungttm@gmail.com

The short version

Tailor has no accounts, no servers of its own, and no advertising. Your resumes and the job descriptions you paste are created, stored, and analyzed only on your iPhone. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

  • Resumes you create (contact details, work history, education, skills, certifications)
  • Job descriptions you paste and the match results calculated from them
  • Your subscription state, as reported by Apple's App Store

This data lives in the app's local storage on your device. If you delete the app, it is deleted with it. We cannot see it, back it up, or recover it.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or resume contents.
  • We do not run analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell personal data. There is no personal data to sell — we never receive it.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. To validate your subscription and unlock Pro features, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription infrastructure provider. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-user identifier and purchase/transaction information from Apple so it can confirm your entitlement. RevenueCat does not receive your resumes, job descriptions, or any document content. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous sales and download statistics through App Store Connect (for example, total downloads by country). These reports cannot identify you.

Children

Tailor is a job-search tool intended for adults seeking employment. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
