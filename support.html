Frequently asked questions

Where is my resume stored?
Only on your iPhone. Tailor has no accounts and no cloud — your resumes and job descriptions never leave your device. If you delete the app, your resumes are deleted with it, so export a PDF of anything you want to keep.

What does the Match Score mean?
It's an estimate of how many of a job posting's key skills and terms also appear in your resume, weighted by how often the posting mentions them. 80+ is a strong match for that specific posting; 60–79 is a real match with fixable gaps. It's a diagnostic to guide tailoring — every employer configures their hiring software differently, so no tool can guarantee a pass.

Why does the app tell me not to add keywords I don't have?
Because the score only gets you to a human. A keyword you can't speak to in an interview will cost you the offer. Add the posting's wording for skills you genuinely have — that's what tailoring means.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tailor → Cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the current period. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I restore a purchase on a new phone?
Open Tailor → Go Pro → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

The PDF looks plain. Is that on purpose?
Yes. Applicant tracking systems garble columns, tables, and graphics. Tailor's single-column, text-based PDFs are designed to be extracted perfectly — and the app proves it: the Export screen shows how many characters an ATS can extract from your actual file.

Which file name should I use?
Firstname-Lastname-Resume.pdf — the app suggests it automatically. Some systems read the file name, and recruiters always see it.
